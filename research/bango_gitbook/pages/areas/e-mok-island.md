# E-Mok Island

<figure><img src="/files/QoLoaixrZPb1FQf7orfz" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> Bango possessed the knowledge to control the dragon—an ancient and immensely powerful creature. In the beginning, the dragon was not hostile toward humans, but under Bango's influence, it turned dangerous. Having lived for countless ages, the dragon held an incredible reservoir of energy.
>
> To prevent the dragon from using its power against him, Bango built a temple and placed one of the dragon's nails inside it, ensuring that its energy remained contained. However, after Bango's defeat in the War of Tak-Rok, he lost control of the dragon. The energy stored within the nail began to seep out, spreading across the land. This unleashed power caused other organisms to mutate into monstrous creatures, which soon began to ravage nature itself.
>
> In response, Hanin ordered the destruction of all the dragon’s nails scattered throughout the nation. Most were successfully destroyed, thanks to the vigilance of the magicians serving the Son of the Sky, who tirelessly worked to keep the dark energy from reaching Geum-Oh Mountain.
>
> One day, while searching for remnants of the dragon’s power, the magicians discovered a dangerous energy source. The Son of the Sky dispatched an observer group, which tracked the energy to E-Mok Island—a place already infamous for rumors of mutated dragons. Upon investigation, they found four dragon’s nails buried deep within the island, with a ruined dragon temple standing at the center.
>
> When they attempted to destroy the nails, they were forced to retreat due to relentless attacks from the mutated dragons, known as E-Moogies. The island had already fallen under the control of these creatures, along with an army of demon soldiers. To make matters worse, E-Mok Island was too far from Geum-Oh Mountain, beyond the reach of the Son of the Sky’s divine power.
>
> Realizing the magnitude of the threat, the Son of the Sky resolved to gather warriors from across the nation to launch a decisive mission—to destroy every last one of the dragon’s nails and put an end to the spreading corruption
>
> \~ InixSoft 2009

### Certification System

Regular characters cannot stay at E-Mok for longer than 1 hour daily. There is no level restriction to enter the island.

There is character level modifier that increase daily limit:

| Character Level | Add. Minutes | Total             |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| 60+             | 15           | 1 hour 15 minutes |
| 70+             | 30           | 1 hour 30 minutes |

And honor grade modifier:

| Honor Grade | Add. Minutes | Total             |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| Corporal    | 10           | 1 hour 10 minutes |
| Captain     | 20           | 1 hour 20 minutes |
| Colonel     | 30           | 1 hour 30 minutes |

Meaning that maximum available time is 2 hours (for Lv. 70+ Colonels). To increase E-Mook time use Certificate of Son of The Sky. Entering E-Mok at any time grants player +10% EXP & Zamogeon buff.

### Monster with Skills

Small and Twisted Brown E-Moogi have Movement Speed Decrease curse. The buff is stackable, meaning that the curse grade increases when applied multiple times. To cancel gradual effect run away and wait for the buff to cancel itself. It is possible to use Buff Remover to cancel the curse. The curse application chance is level based, and chance decreases as character level increases.

### E-Mook Boss Scenario

Once in a week - **every Sunday, 19:00 server time** (type /servertime to get current time), there is a chance to capture the appearance of E-Mook bosses. Destroy 4 Nails of Dragon in order to summon Thousand Year Old E-Moogi dragon.

<figure><img src="/files/rcN8jEZpjGy6CEc5C2x9" alt=""><figcaption><p>Thousand Year Old E-Moogi</p></figcaption></figure>

The Thousand year old E-Moogi never moves and has huge attack range. It has 5 skills:

* ranged stackable speed decrease curse (area of attack) (max G9)
* ranged stackable poison curse (area of effect) (max G9)
* "blast" which deals damage in range (area of effect)
* monster summoning skill - number of monsters depends on monster health
* goes underground and comes up in different place

After the boss is defeated, Ghost of Dragon boss appears:

<figure><img src="/files/sQgFZsBLpO0zm0spGf3E" alt=""><figcaption><p>Ghost of Dragon</p></figcaption></figure>

The Ghost of Dragon is able to walk and has shorter attack range. It has 5 skills:

* ranged stackable speed decrease curse (area of effect) (max G9)
* ranged stackable poison curse (area of effect) (max G9)
* "tail" ranged attack which deals damage in range **behind the monster** (area of effect)
* "breath" ranged attack which deals damage in range **in front of the monster** (area of effect)
* "lightball" which summons child monsters - number of monsters depends on monster health

### Drop list

* G62 Golden Weapons (including int/wis version of a stick)
* G60 Golden Main Parts
* G59 Weapons
* G56 Weapons
* G55 Armor

Note that Ghost of Dragon is subject to the new [New Drop System](/kalonline-2012-content/systems/new-drop-system.md) system. It means that aggro party does not take all the drops, but everyone who deals damage has a chance to get some loot.
