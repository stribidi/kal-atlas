# Doggebi Tower

<figure><img src="/files/rYvrBDAP22GHfNwfJCLc" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> On the top of the tower, the Doggebi Lord appears which is a gigantic boss monster, and more like a group mission. The ‘Doggebi Lord’ only can be summoned by collecting the 8 pieces of ‘Bronze Mirror’ and place them in the each Stone Monument. The ‘Doggebi Lord’ is a powerful monster so many players have to cooperate and concentrate their powers to defeat the ‘Doggebi Lord’.
>
> When the ‘Doggebi Lord’ is summoned, the tower will start to shake and the 5 minutes ceremony will begin.
>
> When the ‘Doggebi Lord’ is summoned, it will disappear after 60 minutes so players must defeat him in 60 minutes.
>
> In addition, ‘Doggebi Lord’ uses various magical attacks (teleporting, area targeting attack, summoning guards and etc) and absorbs Evil Spirit for healing his HP so all players must use wise strategy to defeat him.

### Rules and restrictions&#x20;

* The boss scenario is **not instanced**, therefore **kill steal** is **allowed**
* PK is **restricted** (not possible to use assasin mask effect)
* Boss is **not subject** to [New Drop System](/kalonline-2012-content/systems/new-drop-system.md), meaning drop distribution is bound to a single tank party
* All the monster abilities remain present as seen in 2009 global kal, including teleportation to side-rooms. Most of the skills damage is nerfed to balance for smaller player base. Teleportation affects only small group of people, and never a tanker.

### Drop Rate

Drop count **is not** related to EXP/DROP rate (unlike the other bosses). The reason behind that is a fact that EXP/DROP rate (usually refered as "server progression speed") is already speeding up mirror collection process. It means that on high-rate versions of Bango (for example x40), there are 40x more summons per month, meaning 40x more drops per month, which matches server progression speed. On Bango version x1, there are not many lord kills, therefore less drops per month.

### Loot

* 100% chance for at least 1x G65 weapon (basic one)
* a chance to get custom G65 weapon (including int/wis version of a stick) - **max 4;** but medium loot chance (25% for each draw)
* multiple G56 and G59 weapons (**max 24**, but the count is usually below 8)
* max 10x Lord Horn (50% chance for each draw)
* Zamogeons

### Disclaimers

Please plan your lord kill ahead. Player whose mirrors are put in place is not owner of the final drops. Administrator is never involved in manual drop distribution in case of kill steal by competing players.
