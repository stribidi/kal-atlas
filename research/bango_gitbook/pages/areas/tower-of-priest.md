## Elemental Masters

Open world semi-bosses with 6 hour respawn time. The schedule is:

* 3:40 AM
* 9:40 AM
* 3:40 PM
* 9:40 PM

### Earth element master (Floor 3)

Possible loot:

* G65 Boots of all classes

Skills:

* TBD

### Wood element master (Floor 4)

Possible loot:

* G65 Shorts of all classes
* G65 Knight & Mage boots

Skills:

* TBD

### Water element master (Floor 6)

Possible loot:

* G65 Shorts of all classes
* G65 Archer & Knight boots

Skills:

* TBD

### Fire element master (Floor 8)

Possible loot:

* G65 Chest of all classes
* G65 Mage & Archer boots

Skills:

* TBD

### Iron element master (Floor 9)

Possible loot:

* G65 Chest of all classes
* G65 Knight & Archer & Mage boots

Skills:

* TBD

G65 main parts include multiple variations of the same item (stat-wise).

All masters have very slight chance of G55 armor and G60 side parts.

All masters loot specific essences and elements.
