# Demon Queen

<figure><img src="/files/BPWtRdFnPEF91BKlCz1r" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="/files/fiZ5MT0309H2OIM3MSOc" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> This is the demon who controls the Demon Nest. Generally, she sticks to the Nest but when she feels any threat, she separates from the Nest and protects herself. When she's separates with the Nest, the production of demon eggs stops. However, she still has ability to protect herself even if she's stick together with the Nest. She attacks with her tentacles through the subterrain and blossoms some kind of flame plants.
>
> **When she is together with the Nest**
>
> Only the upper body can be seen but she still can attack from the Nest. Her tentacles pierce from underground but when she's together with the Nest, she won't move.
>
> **When she is separated with the Nest**
>
> She appears to have upper body of a woman with the wings. She has lower body and the wings of an insect. She always flies and barely goes down to the ground.

### Rules and restrictions&#x20;

* The boss spawn time is once in a week, every Saturday, 19:00 Server Time (type /servertime to check current time)
* The boss scenario is **not instanced**, therefore **kill steal** is **allowed**
* PK is available (it is **possible** to use assasin mask effect)
* This World Boss is **subject** to [New Drop System](/kalonline-2012-content/systems/new-drop-system.md), meaning drops are distributed to multiple parties, based on hostility
* All the monster abilities remain present as seen in 2009 global kal. Most of the skills damage is nerfed to balance for smaller player base.

### Loot

* G60 Darkness Armor
* G62 Darkness Weapon
* Zamogeons

### Disclaimers

Please plan your battle ahead. Administrator is never involved in manual drop distribution in case of kill steal by competing players.
