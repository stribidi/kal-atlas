## Call of Defense

<div align="left"><figure><img src="/files/ZtiwLjC0Uy0xswuHhhnb" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### Description

Increasing group members' defense. Its effectiveness disappears if another chant is used. Once character acquires third job change, 2 chants are effective at the same time. [Effect disappears when party member gets out of range.](#user-content-fn-1)[^1]

{% hint style="info" %}
The disappearance when party member gets out of range is modification to classic 2004 setting. This is changed to follow Inixsoft's 2009 patch.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Another modification is that the call is re-applied once party players get back in range of Commander. Calls enabled by the player who is dead will be cancelled for entire party.
{% endhint %}

#### Level Limit

* G1 - 51 Lv.
* G2 - 57 Lv.
* G3 - 63 Lv.

#### Required Condition

None

#### Mana Consumption

0

#### Close Defense %

```
9 + SKILL_LEVEL * 7
```

#### Far Defense %

```
9 + SKILL_LEVEL * 3
```

#### Buff Time

Forever

#### Range

300[^2]

{% hint style="info" %}
It was modified to follow 2009 update. In classic 2004 setting it was 64.
{% endhint %}

#### Cast time

Instant

#### Cooldown time

10 seconds

***
