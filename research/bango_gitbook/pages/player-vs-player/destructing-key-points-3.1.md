# Destructing Key Points (3.1)

## Registration

To participate, guild leader (or sub-leader) needs to register by talking to Destructing Key Points NPC, Blue Doggebi. He is located nearby Doggebi Tower (D3). Shortest path is by talking to Guide of Doggebi which teleports you to D3 enterance, or walk by foot from Temporary Forest.

Registration window is open at all times, up to the guild selection phase (1 hour before battle). Requirements are:

* minimum guild level 2
* 30.000 Zamogeons

Selected guilds will be announced to everyone 1 hour before battle begins (6 PM). Please use this time to prepare for the battle, since it begins immediately with no further preparation time.

Destructing Key Points (3.1) scenario takes place at 7 PM server time every Monday. It lasts 1 hour.

| Phase                        | Time                 |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Guild registration window    | Anytime              |
| Guild selection announcement | Monday, 6 PM         |
| Battle                       | Monday, 7 PM \~ 8 PM |

## Battle Start

When the battle begins, all players are teleported to the arena safezone. Each team has separate safezone. There is no more waiting time, users can immediately teleport to fightning place using Doggebi NPC. It is not possible to go back to this place by foot. This is also place for defeated players to resurrect automatically.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/files/SWZVctfxE91AzNP50vfW" alt=""><figcaption><p>Safe zone, resurrection place</p></figcaption></figure>

## General Information

* when player dies during the battle, EXP is not reduced; player is automatically resurrected in guild safezone; it is possible to use Revival Skill, however Rebirth Scrolls are restricted
* absent guild players will be teleported in once become online; it is not possible to exit the arena completely before the scenario ends

## Win Condition

Guild with more total points win the battle. To acquire points, guild must destroy opponent's towers. The towers can be upgraded. There is no additional point for PK (player killing).

## Tower Destruction

The map is divided into blue and red team sides. There are small (circle) and big (square) towers on both sides of the map. There is one Giant Tower (yellow square). The no-fight zone is marked with red lines.

<div data-full-width="true"><figure><img src="/files/0JspdF4Dbzzk1Wkls04i" alt=""><figcaption><p>Map Description</p></figcaption></figure></div>

The map is divided into *Left Side* and *Right Side* depending on guild safezone perspective. When the battle starts, each guild can only attack opponent's towers on the *Left Side* of map. Only after the first tower on the *Left Side* is destroyed, guild can proceed with any small (circle) or big (square) opponent's towers.

After at least one tower on the *Right Side* is destroyed, guild can proceed to attack towers on the center (big towers marked squares in the infographic).

Giant tower (yellow square) can be attacked by *both guilds*, only when 4 of small towers of one of the guilds are destroyed. Points are obtained by the guild which deals more damage to the tower.

| Kind of Tower                                           | Condition of Attack                                    | Points                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Towers on the *Left Side* of the friendly force's base  | Always possible                                        | 2                                  |
| Towers on the *Right Side* of the friendly force's base | If one of the towers on the *Left Side* is destroyed   | 2                                  |
| Big Towers                                              | If one of the towers on the *Right Side* is destroyed  | 5                                  |
| Giant Tower                                             | If 4 or more of small towers of one team are destroyed | 3 (for the guild with more damage) |
| Upgraded Tower                                          |                                                        | Normal Points + 1                  |

## Tower Upgrade

Guild members can upgrade own towers. It is only possible, when the tower has not yet been attacked (when the HP is full).

Upgraded towers increase max HP by 50%. But upgrading a tower comes with a cost:

* upgrade item (Upgraded Model of Tower) costs 40.000 Zamogeons (purchasable in Narootuh)
* upgraded tower gives enemy 1 more point when destroyed

## Battle End

Battle ends either when guilds run out of time (1 hour), or specific conditions are all met:

* Giant Tower (yellow square) is destroyed
* All towers of one side (blue or red) are destroyed

Then, the guild with more points win.

## Honor Rewards

The honor points and reward points are delivered immediately, upon Tower Destruction. There is no additional reward for battle winning, loosing nor tie. However, there is Honor Point & Reward Point for PvP kill - depending on target Honor Rank.

| Reason                  | Honor Point | Reward Point |
| ----------------------- | ----------- | ------------ |
| Winner                  | 0           | 0            |
| Loser                   | 0           | 0            |
| Tie                     | 0           | 0            |
| Small Tower Destruction | 300         | 3000         |
| Big Tower Destruction   | 500         | 5000         |
| Giant Tower Destruction | 800         | 8000         |

> IMPORTANT: The rewards are adjusted according to server EXP rate. For example server with EXP 3x, has 3x higher honor rewards.
