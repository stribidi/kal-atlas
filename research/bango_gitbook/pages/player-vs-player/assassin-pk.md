# Assassin (PK)

There is original assassin mask that lasts 1 hour, but there is also custom version of a mask:

<figure><img src="/files/sMRTgcQWX2ZSnbh1WiHH" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Restrictions

* it is **not possible** to use the mask during PvP such as: Castle War, Champion, Battlefield, GvG
* it is **not possible** to use the mask inside Doggebi Tower Floor 9 (Doggebi Lord is free from PvP)
* it is **not possible** to use the mask during Tower of Priest I'lyer Scenario

Player PK (assassin) is **allowed** during world bosses such as Thousand Year Emoogi, Demon Queen and Elemental Masters.

### RB-killing rules

This is very old term coming from the old times where players did not get blessing buff after using Rebirth Scroll. RB-killing meant to **sit and wait for dead player to use rebirth scroll, just to immediately kill the player again**.

With blessing buff, the player is free to teleport out from the play area before assassin deals damage once again. This means it is not possible to RB-kill anyone, since defeated player can always freely leave the PK area before getting killed again.

### Assassin death penalty

There is no penalty when dying to assassin. (other than loosing EXP when not using Rebirth Scroll)

There is **10% chance to drop a random item from your inventory to the ground** when dying while using assasin mask. Disclaimer: the items will never be returned if you die "by mistake" when leaving safezone.
