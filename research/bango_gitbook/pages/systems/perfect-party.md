# Perfect Party

Depending on content unlocked, party consist of maximum 6 or 8 players.

| Thief Included | Thief Excluded |
| -------------- | -------------- |
| 8 players      | 6 players      |

Perfect party is special condition that increases EXP by 20%.

#### Condition required

* party is full of second (or higher) job
* all the player jobs are unique (there is no character pair with the same second job)
* all players stay in close range
