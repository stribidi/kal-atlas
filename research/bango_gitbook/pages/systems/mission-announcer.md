# Mission Announcer

## How to use?

Visit Narootuh or Temporary Fort and search for Mission Announcer NPC. The merchant sells mission scrolls which describe quest requirements.

<figure><img src="/files/IIFPMW9EtOU3MZcMSNmR" alt=""><figcaption><p>Mission Announcer at Temporary Fort</p></figcaption></figure>

1. Obtain required monster heads or personal items
2. Buy the scroll
3. Right click and press \[completed]
4. Receive EXP and Zamogeons

<figure><img src="/files/LtnYzXhckSND0r4XPOva" alt=""><figcaption><p>Quest Window</p></figcaption></figure>

The special effect indicates the quest has been finished.
