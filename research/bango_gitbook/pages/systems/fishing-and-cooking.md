# Fishing & Cooking

## Fishing

### Buying a fishing hook

This can be done in every town in the game. Talk to the merchant to buy the hooks. Setting up a fishing rod consumes 1 hook.

<figure><img src="/files/19F9BJvjuIviEzJnJlsI" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Moving to fishing islands

It is possible to fish in the Geum-Oh islands. Use one of the NPCs nearby to teleport to the location.

<figure><img src="/files/fSGFxV6g5YjRGgBGtz1t" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Limitations

It is possible to fish only 10 minutes a day. The fishing speed is significantly increased from 10 minutes to 12 seconds. It is possible to speed up the process by hand, when using Stone of Fishery. It does not increase fishing count per day. All fishes are bound to the character.

The amount of fishes per catch is increased significantly. Please find the table below:

|                                                  | Global Kal | Bango                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Number of fishes per catch activation            | 1          | 6                                    |
| Time to complete one catch activation            | 10 minutes | 12 seconds                           |
| Time to complete one catch with Stone of Fishery | 5 minutes  | 6 seconds                            |
| Fishing limitation per day                       | none       | 10 minutes (5 with Stone of Fishery) |
| Items are character bound                        | no         | yes                                  |

That makes it possible to obtain 300 fishes daily, with just 10 minute time span.

## Cooking

After you caught your daily dose of bound fishes, it is a time to cook. Talk to the Mistress in Narootuh.
