# Monster Summoning Scrolls

### Where to buy?

In Narootuh Merchant, Gae-Yoo

<figure><img src="/files/UJMlSz5jTdXttTYVkmDt" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Death lure prevention

In order to prevent scrollers to death lure innocent players, monsters are automatically targeting the caster upon item use. Once monsters loose agro, they automatically blob. To prevent random blobbing simply use the scrolls very close to the player.

Monsters disappear after 60 seconds.

### How to use?

Right click the item in inventory or move to skill bar to use keyboard. Select the ground where the monsters should be spawned.

Scrolls cannot be used inside dungeons, on castle war or PvP,&#x20;
