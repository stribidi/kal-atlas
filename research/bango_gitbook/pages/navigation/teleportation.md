# Teleportation

## Cities

The easiest way to teleport to different cities is to use chat commands:

<figure><img src="/files/W0VN6klbdN5xyETG4HIQ" alt=""><figcaption><p>Teleport commands are available for all players</p></figcaption></figure>

Available commands:

* `/naro` - Narootuh
* `/cargo` - Jook-Suh Cargo Station
* `/mine` - Geum-Oh Mine
* `/fort` - Temporary Fort of Geum-Ohee Castle
* `/bird` - Pub of The Giant Bird
* `/cop` - City of Priest

Another option is to use **teleportation scrolls**, which **do not get removed once you use it**. It means it is enough to have 1 teleportation scroll in inventory at all times.
