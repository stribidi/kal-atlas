# KAL Online — Imperial Mixing System Guide

The **Imperial Mixing system** is a weapon enhancement mechanic that lets you upgrade weapons and add elemental effects through the **Mix Master NPC**, found in the city's **Pub of the Giant Bird**.

---

## Prerequisites

- Your main weapon must be **Grade 40 or higher**
- You need **Zamogeons** (in-game currency) — every attempt has a cost
- The Mix Master NPC offers three options:
  - **Upgrading a Weapon** — assigns an elemental stone effect to a weapon
  - **Making a Weapon (Imperial)** — raises the weapon to a higher tier
  - **Mixing Item List** — shows exactly which materials are needed per level

---

## The Two Core Paths

### 1. Elemental Stone Upgrading (Adding Skills)

This path adds an elemental skill to your weapon across up to **3 levels** of power.

| Level | Required Materials | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Lv. 1** | Main weapon + Weapon + Elemental Stone | Element effect added (e.g., 5% Flame damage) |
| **Lv. 2** | Main weapon (Lv. 1) + Weapon + Armor + Elemental Stone | Effect upgraded (e.g., 10% Flame damage) |
| **Lv. 3** | Main weapon (Lv. 2) + Weapon + Armor + 2× Rare Stones | All previous effects removed, new strong effect applied |

#### Available Elemental Stones (Standard)

| Stone | Lv. 1 Effect | Lv. 2 Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Stone of Flame** | Flame dmg 5% chance | Flame dmg 10% chance |
| **Stone of Ice** | Ice dmg 5% chance | Ice dmg 10% chance |
| **Stone of Lightning** | Lightning dmg 5% chance | Lightning dmg 10% chance |
| **Stone of Poison** | Poison 5% chance | Poison 10% chance |
| **Stone of Paralysis** | Paralysis 5% chance | Paralysis 10% chance |
| **Stone of Strength** | HP +10%, MP –5% | HP +20%, MP –10% |
| **Stone of Skill** | HP –5%, MP +10% | HP –10%, MP +20% |

#### Lv. 3 Rare Stones (Item Shop / Special Drop)

For **Lv. 3**, you must use two of the same rare stone. The stats on your server are:

| Stone | Stats Granted |
|---|---|
| **Stone of Mystery** | PDMin +50 <br> PDMax +50 <br> MDMin +50 <br> MDMax +50 |
| **Stone of Demon's Blood** | Strength +6 <br> Health +10 <br> Intelligence +6 <br> Wisdom +10 <br> Agility +6 <br> HP +50 <br> MP +50 |

---

### 2. Imperial Making (Weapon Tier Upgrade)

This path creates a new upgraded "Imperial" weapon while retaining your original weapon's stats and job restrictions. The result typically has higher base damage and a different appearance.

- **Levels 1–2**: Main Item + Weapon + Armor + Elemental Stone
- **Level 3**: Main Item (must be Lv. 2) + Weapon + Armor + Rare Material (Stone of Mystery or Stone of Demon's Blood)

---

## Failure & Risk Management

Every mixing attempt carries a **success rate that varies by weapon grade**. Key things to know:

- On **failure**, the main weapon loses **5–10 Endurance**; if the weapon doesn't have enough endurance to absorb the loss, the attempt is blocked.
- The **elemental stone and Zamogeons are always consumed**, regardless of success or failure.
- **Endurance can be repaired** using Fixing Tools purchased from merchants.
