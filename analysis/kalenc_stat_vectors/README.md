# Decoded stat vectors

`ita_rev` (ITEMS) and `pre_rev` (PREFIXES) in the read-only KalEncyclopedia
exports are `|`-delimited vectors whose positions were previously unknown, so
the columns were carried as opaque strings. The position names were recovered
from `modGlobal::strStats` inside `KalEncyclopedia.exe`.

Generated by `tools\kalenc_extract\decode_stat_vectors.py`, which reads the slot
names back out of the dumped IL rather than restating them, so a different build
cannot silently disagree with this data.

| File | Rows | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `STAT_SLOTS.csv` | 27 | slot index → stat name, with provenance |
| `ITEM_STATS.csv` | 3,537 | one row per populated stat on each of 704 items |
| `PREFIX_STATS.csv` | 229 | one row per populated stat on each of 102 prefixes |

Every row keeps its `raw_vector` beside the decode, so the decode is checkable
without re-reading `data\`.

## Known limit, deliberately surfaced

**31 positions across nine Mix Master stones run past slot 26 and contain
commas.** Stone of Flame, Ice, Lightening, Poison, Paralyzis, Strength, Skill,
Mystery and Demons Blood carry **two comma-separated vectors in the 28-slot
`strStatsMix` order** (whose tail differs: 25 = Enemy Defense, 26 = HP,
27 = Mana), not one vector in the 27-slot order.

Those rows are emitted with their raw index and a `slotNN` placeholder name, and
the tool prints the count, rather than being dropped or force-fitted. What the
two vectors mean — most likely a range or a pair of outcomes — is **to confirm**
against `Imperial_Mixing_System_Guide.md` and `frmMix`.

Provenance of everything here is `kalencyclopedia-assembly` over the read-only
2011-era export. **It is not Bango data.** Bango's own item records
(`bango_data\ITEMS.csv`, `bango_specialty_json`) remain the current authority
for items that exist in both; this table's value is coverage of fields Bango
does not expose and corroboration where they overlap.
