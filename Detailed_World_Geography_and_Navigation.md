## 3. Navigation & Corridors (Routes)

### Route A: Narootuh ↔ Cargo Station

- **Path:** Indirect "U" shape to bypass the bay at O13.
- **Directions:**
  1. Exit **Narootuh (P11)** East into **Q12**.
  2. Traverse **Q12** East into **Q13**.
  3. Turn North-East at **Q13** to enter **P14**.
  4. Walk North from **P14** to arrive at **Cargo Station (O14)**.
- **Estimated Distance:** ~6 Grid Squares (**528 ws**).

### Route B: Cargo Station ↔ Geuh Mine

- **Path:** Northbound linear valley.
- **Directions:**
  1. Exit **Cargo Station (O14)** North to **N14**.
  2. Pass West through **N13** (Checkpoint 3).
  3. Travel North-West through **M13** (Checkpoint 4) and **L13**.
  4. Enter **L12** and continue North to **Geuh Mine (K12)**.
- **Estimated Distance:** ~5 Grid Squares (**440 ws**).

### Route C: Narootuh ↔ Fort

- **Path:** Southern expedition.
- **Directions:**
  1. Exit **Narootuh (P11)** East into **Q12**.
  2. Turn South into **R12**.
  3. Continue South through **S12** and **S13**.
  4. Turn South-East into **T13** to arrive at **Fort (T14)**.
- **Estimated Distance:** ~7 Grid Squares (**616 ws**).

### Route D: Geuh Mine ↔ Pub

- **Path:** Short Western hook.
- **Directions:**
  1. Exit **Geuh Mine (K12)** West into **K11**.
  2. Turn North in **K11** to arrive at **Pub (J11)**.
- **Estimated Distance:** ~2 Grid Squares (**176 ws**).
